\section{Main objective}

The aim of this thesis is to develop a graph neural network models that could be applied in two tasks:
a) to predict the presence of hidden links in a causal graph, 
b) to predict the values of endpoints for entities based on tabular data represented as a causal graph.

The two tasks are complementary ... [we will add here how the two tasks relate to each other in medicine + picture that shows it]
The models will be trained and evaluated on synthetic datasets, as well as on real-world datasets. The performance of the models will be compared with baseline methods, and the results will be analyzed to understand the strengths and limitations of the proposed approach.
